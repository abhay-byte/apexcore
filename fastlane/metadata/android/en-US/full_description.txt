ApexCore optimizes your device's memory for gaming by deep-freezing resource-heavy background processes. Using elevated privileges via Shizuku or Root, it completely stops background processes to ensure maximum hardware resources are allocated to your games.

Features:
- Advanced Freeze Framework: Pluggable backends supporting Shizuku, Root access, and accessibility fallback.
- Performance HUD: A draggable overlay service displaying real-time FPS, RAM pressure, and CPU load.
